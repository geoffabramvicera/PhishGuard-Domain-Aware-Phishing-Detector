## Public Suffix List

`public_suffix_list.dat` is a bundled snapshot of the Public Suffix List used for offline parsing of registered domains. The upstream project is maintained at https://github.com/publicsuffix/list and published at https://publicsuffix.org/list/.

The Public Suffix List data is distributed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. See https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/ and the upstream project for the current terms and update process. The local snapshot may become stale over time, so refresh it from the upstream list periodically if domain parsing accuracy is important.
